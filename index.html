CORPORATE OVERVIEW

Delbuster Enterprise: Driving Value Across Sectors

Delbuster Enterprise is a diversified, multi-sector conglomerate built on the principles of strategic opportunity, operational resilience, and long-term economic value creation. Headquartered in Nigeria, our portfolio spans high-impact industries designed to foster sustainable growth, facilitate cross-border trade, and build enduring enterprise value across emerging markets.

The Pointer serves as the flagship media asset of Delbuster Enterprise. As an independent, investigative media powerhouse, The Pointer is dedicated to delivering uncompromising journalism, evidence-based reporting, and sharp, unfiltered commentary on the critical political, economic, and social forces shaping Nigeria and the global landscape.

By bridging commercial excellence with public-interest journalism, Delbuster Enterprise continues to pioneer solutions, empower decision-makers, and elevate accountability across every market we touch.
